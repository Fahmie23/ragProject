ANSWER:
When there is a positive match with a designated person, the reporting institution must report the information set out in Appendix I, which includes UNSCR Permanent Ref No, Customer Name, Address, NRIC/Passport No., Intermediary Name (if reporting on a group basis), Branch maintaining the account/facility, Account No., Account Facility/Type, Date services given, Account/Facility Status on the date of freezing, Date account frozen, Account Balance, Related Parties, and Remarks.
